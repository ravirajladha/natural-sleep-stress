# Natural Sleep & Stress Test (PHP MVC)

Natural Sleep & Stress Test is a web application built with PHP MVC, designed to help users assess their sleep quality and stress levels. By answering a series of questions, users receive a score that indicates their sleep and stress health, along with recommendations for improvement. This tool aims to promote better sleep habits and stress management for overall well-being.

## Features

- **Sleep and Stress Assessment**: Answer questions to evaluate your sleep quality and stress levels.
- **Scoring System**: Receive a score with categorized feedback based on your responses.
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Get tailored advice to improve sleep and reduce stress.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Simple and intuitive design for easy navigation.
- **MVC Architecture**: Organized codebase using the Model-View-Controller pattern for scalability.

## Prerequisites

- PHP >= 7.4
- MySQL or MariaDB
- Apache (e.g., via XAMPP)
- Composer for dependency management
- Git

## Scoring Ranges

After completing the test, your score will fall into one of the following categories:

- **0 - 25**: Healthy Sleep - Your sleep and stress levels are in a healthy range. Keep up the good habits!
- **26 - 50**: Moderate Sleep - Your sleep and stress levels are moderate. Consider implementing some improvements.
- **51 - 75**: Needs Improvement - Your sleep and stress levels need attention. Follow the recommendations to enhance your well-being.
- **76 - 100**: Good Practice Required - Significant improvements are needed in your sleep and stress management. Prioritize the suggested practices.


## Usage

- **Take the Test**: Answer a series of questions about your sleep habits and stress levels.
- **View Results**: Receive a score and see which category you fall into (Healthy Sleep, Moderate Sleep, etc.).
- **Follow Recommendations**: Implement the personalized tips to improve your sleep quality and reduce stress.
- **Track Progress**: Retake the test periodically to monitor improvements in your sleep and stress health.
